# Overtonium 1.18.0

A 32-partial additive synthesizer laid out like a 32-channel mixer. Every channel is one sine oscillator locked to a harmonic of the played note, and every channel has its own tuning, envelope and modulation.

## What is new in 1.18.0

**A partial can ring another (#144).** The AMP MOD shape menu has a Channel submenu that lists every partial. Choose one and its wave multiplies this channel's sample by sample, which makes the sum and the difference of their two frequencies. Every partial is a whole multiple of the note, so the new tones are too, and the result stays in tune. DEPTH goes from none of it at 0% to ring modulation at 100%, where the partial's own tone is gone. The source passes on its envelope, velocity, aftertouch, KEYBOARD row and its own AMP MOD, but not its fader or mute, so a partial can be muted or pulled all the way down and still modulate. When the sum would come near the top of the audio band, the depth fades out so nothing folds back out of tune.

**A partial can move another's pitch (#145).** The PITCH MOD shape menu has the same Channel submenu. There the other partial moves this one's phase, as FM synthesizers do, which spreads it into pairs of tones either side of it, all whole multiples of the note. DEPTH is then the modulation index, from 0 to 12, and the popup says so. Partials can be stacked as an FM synthesizer stacks its operators. The index is held back where the tones would pass the top of the audio band, and host automation still shows the depth in cents, a hundred to each step of the index.

**Feedback (#146, #147).** In the pitch menu, a partial's own place is Feedback, which moves its phase by its own wave, as the feedback operator on a DX7 does. As the index rises the sine turns towards a sawtooth, and from about 2 it turns to noise. Below 2 the index is held where its harmonics would fold back, so a low partial reaches about 2 and one at 10 kHz stays close to a sine. The offset feedback puts in is taken out, as the DX7's output does.

**The noise channel as a source, and as a target (#148).** Both Channel submenus end with Noise. On amplitude, noise spreads a partial into a band of noise around its own pitch, from a touch of breath to a whistle. On pitch, it makes the partial rough and unsteady. With STRING up, the source is the string, so the new tones stay in tune and die away as the string does. The noise channel's own AMP MOD menu has the Channel submenu too, so a partial can move the noise up around its pitch. Following the fundamental, the noise pulses at the note's pitch.

**Loops play a sample at a time (#150).** Two partials can follow each other, by AMP MOD, by PITCH MOD or both, and so can a partial and the noise channel. The channels in a loop play together a sample at a time, as a DX7 plays its operators, so the first of them hears the others one sample late. Round a loop, each passes on less of its own ring the deeper the other links are. A link at no depth counts as no link at all, so turning DEPTH down to nothing sounds the same as choosing a shape.

**The mixer shows what modulates what (#151, #152).** A small lamp beside a channel's number lights while that channel modulates another. Pointing at a channel lights the numbers of the channels it takes from and gives to, each in its own colour, and a loop lights as one. The shape screen shows the source's number on a two-digit display in the source's colour, FB for feedback and the noise trace for the noise channel. RATE goes dark when a channel is the source, since the source sets the rate. A muted channel that still modulates one you can hear keeps its knobs and screens lit, and only its fader and level go dark.

**The noise channel is warm white.** Its colour was a cool grey, close to the captions around it. It is now a warm white, so it stands apart from both the captions and the partials.

**A darker panel.** The channels and the sidebar are a little darker, the unlit ticks around the knobs are fainter, and the row names and captions are brighter, so the controls and their names stand out more.

**Three new presets.** _FM KarplusStrong_ is a plucked string from the noise channel, with partials ringing and moving each other for a more synthetic edge. _FM Perc Piano_ goes from very soft at low velocity to bright and punchy, full of overtones, at high velocity. _FM String Machine_ uses feedback to make a sawtooth string ensemble. All three are on the FM character. Forty-seven presets in all.

## Fixed

**"Aftertouch from: Mod wheel" still let pressure through (#153).** A note's own pressure, from MPE or from polyphonic aftertouch, always reached the voice, whatever the setting. With "Mod wheel" the wheel is now the only way in, with MPE on or off. The pressure meter on the Playing bar still shows what the keyboard sends.

**The noise channel's meter missed the string (#156).** With STRING up, the meter followed only the short burst that strikes the string, so it fell almost at once while the string was still ringing. It now shows the string as you hear it. The sound is unchanged.

## Under it

**884 parameters,** as in 1.17.0. The new sources went on the end of the shape lists.

**A sweep of 3913 cases before release.** 1.17.0's cases with seventeen new setups for the sources: ring, FM, feedback at 1.9, 2.5 and 12, and the noise channel as source and target, at every sample rate and block size, at both ends of the keyboard, and four presets through six ways of playing. All forty-seven presets went through eight ways of playing. Every case renders finite, bounded audio, every case meant to sound makes sound, and the whole sweep ran again under the address and undefined behaviour sanitizers.

## Upgrading from 1.17.0

**Automation of the shape menus lands differently.** A host stores a choice as a fraction of the list's length. On the 32 channels, the AMP MOD shape list grows from 7 choices to 40 and the PITCH MOD shape list from 8 to 41, and the noise channel's AMP MOD shape list from 7 to 39. A lane drawn before 1.18.0 lands on a different shape. Sessions and presets are unaffected, since the new choices went on the end.

**MIDI program changes from 16 up land later.** _FM KarplusStrong_ sorts at program 16, _FM Perc Piano_ at 17 and _FM String Machine_ at 19, so 1.17.0's program 16, _FM Piano_, moves up two and 17 to 43 move up three. A saved session stores the preset's name as well as its number and is unaffected.

**Pressure on "Mod wheel".** If you played MPE or polyphonic aftertouch with "Aftertouch from" on "Mod wheel" and liked what pressure did, choose "Either" for the same result.

## Installing

The [install page](https://benjamindehli.github.io/overtonium/install/#downloads) has a row per file saying who each one is for. In short: take the installer for your platform, or the zip beside it if you have no administrator rights or keep plugins somewhere of your own.

| Platform | Installer              | Formats                      |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| macOS    | `.pkg`, with a chooser | VST3, Audio Unit, Standalone |
| Windows  | `.exe`                 | VST3, Standalone             |
| Linux    | none, use the zip      | VST3, LV2, Standalone        |
