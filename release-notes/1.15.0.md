# Overtonium 1.15.0

A 32-partial additive synthesizer laid out like a 32-channel mixer. Every channel is one sine oscillator locked to a harmonic of the played note, and every channel has its own tuning, envelope and modulation.

## What is new in 1.15.0

**Four characters measured from real instruments.** Until now there were six: the plain sine and five circuits worked out on paper. The four new ones are measured from recordings I made of my own instruments, one sine at a time, so they sound like the instrument they came from rather than like a model of it. CHARACTER now has ten choices.

**Wheel** is a tonewheel organ's generator. The organ has a separate wheel for every pitch, and the wheels do not sound alike, so Wheel picks a set of harmonics by the pitch of each partial. The lowest wheels have a strong second harmonic, about -13 dB on the lowest C, which gets weaker up to A3 and drops away by C4, and above that the wheels are close to pure sines. The wheels also move as they turn: each one by about a cent of pitch and a tenth of a decibel of level once per turn of its shaft, and the motor moves all of them together by about 0.9 cent at 25 Hz. A little of the wheels near each note leaks into it, as on the organ, and that is added too: the same note two octaves down, a fourth down, an octave down and a fifth down, each about 50 dB under the note.

**FM** is one operator of a frequency modulation synthesizer with nothing modulating it, and **PD** is the oscillator of a phase distortion synthesizer with the distortion off. Both are nearly pure sines, with ten harmonics each, all far down. Each note also carries the noise measured under the instrument's own notes, so there is no hiss when nothing plays. On FM most of the hiss above 6.4 kHz is shared by the whole instrument and follows only the loudest note, as it does on the instrument, so it does not build up as you add keys. On PD the instrument's mix bends slightly, which gives each note its second harmonic and gives a chord faint sum and difference tones between its notes, and the character does the same.

**6581** is a sine as a 6581 sound chip would play it. The chip has no sine, but everything that gives it its sound could be measured from the waves it does make. Its 12-bit converter does not step evenly, because each bit is worth about 1.95 times the one below it rather than twice, so the output falls back where many bits change at once. After the converter the output bends slightly. Together they give a sine a second harmonic at about -23 dB and a long tail of faint odd harmonics, carried all the way to the 32nd. Part of the bend is on the chip's mix, so a chord also gets the sum and difference tones its notes make together, and the chip's hiss is there too, the same however many keys are held. The chip was recorded through [SidStation ASID](https://benjamindehli.github.io/sidstation-asid/), my plugin that writes the chip's registers directly, so every setting reached the chip exactly as set.

**FM, PD and 6581 light in their instrument's colour.** The character button lights FM in green, PD in a pale yellow-green and 6581 in a soft blue, taken from the instruments they were measured from. The other characters keep the colours they had.

**Kirnberger III and Vallotti.** Two more temperaments, so the five on a church organ's temperament selector are all here. Kirnberger III keeps the third on the root pure, as meantone does, but has no wolf, so every key can be played. It is a common choice for Bach. Vallotti tempers six fifths in a row by a sixth of the Pythagorean comma each and leaves the rest pure. It is Young's temperament started a fifth lower, and both are offered because players know them by both names. There are now nine temperaments, eight fixed ones and Adaptive.

**The wolf in meantone and Pythagorean is on G#-Eb.** Both have one fifth that cannot be used. It used to be the fifth below the root, so on C, F major and B flat major were out of tune. Organs and harpsichords put it on G#-Eb, which keeps C, F, B flat, G, D and A major in tune, and on C both temperaments now do the same. The wolf moves with the root on any other.

**Three new presets.** _6581 Pulse_ and _6581 Sawtooth_ join _6581 Triangle_ as the chip's three waves. _KarplusStrong_ strikes a lone fundamental and the noise channel's Karplus-Strong string together, with the resonator's piano strings ringing in sympathy. Four presets moved to the new characters: _6581 Triangle_ to 6581, _Drawbar Organ_ to Wheel, _FM Piano_ to FM and _Nylon EP_ to PD. Thirty-eight presets in all.

**A little more top end through the bus drive.** The bus drive's antialiasing took about a decibel off the top octave, around 15 kHz, on every character with a bus stage. It no longer does, so Rail, Diode, Valve, Op-amp, Wheel, PD and 6581 are slightly brighter at the top. Pure, FM and Bulb are unchanged.

## Fixed

**The bus drive could pop on loud notes.** On Valve, Rail and Diode, a loud low note could put a one-sample click into the bus drive's output, which built up into a buzz on held notes. The curve is now exact, so they are clean. Every patch with a bus stage renders very slightly differently because of it, by about one part in a hundred thousand.

**The lamps could show the previous note.** On a channel whose fader is down, the envelope lamps and the pitch mod needle could show the note before for a moment after a new one started, on some notes and not others. They now follow the note you just played.

## Under it

**Still 850 parameters.** The four characters went on the end of CHARACTER's list and the two temperaments on the end of the temperament list, so every saved session and preset keeps the character and temperament it had.

**Built as tables.** Like the circuits, the new characters are band-limited tables worked out at startup, so nothing they add aliases.

**A sweep of 1748 cases before release.** 1.14.0's 832, with every character added among the setups, so each meets every way of playing on four presets and both ends of the rate range. Then each character driven hard at every one of eight sample rates from 8 kHz to 192 kHz and five block sizes from 1 to 4096 samples, the character switched through all ten every 50 ms under ringing notes, every temperament with the root walking round the circle of fifths, and every preset through six ways of playing. Every case renders finite, bounded audio and actually makes sound, and the whole sweep ran again under the address and undefined behaviour sanitizers.

**The docs' clips and pictures are rendered again.** Every clip on the site was rendered from this version, and the window pictures show the lamps as they now behave.

## Upgrading from 1.14.0

**Automation of CHARACTER and the temperament lands differently.** A host stores a choice as a fraction of the list's length. CHARACTER's list grows from six to ten and the temperament list from seven to nine, so a lane drawn before 1.15.0 lands a little further along. Sessions are unaffected, since they store the choice's position in the list, and no existing choice moved.

**Sessions saved with meantone or Pythagorean sound different.** The same root now gives the standard layout, so the wolf is in a different place. Every other temperament is unaffected.

**MIDI program changes from 2 up land later.** _6581 Pulse_ and _6581 Sawtooth_ sort at programs 2 and 3, and _KarplusStrong_ at 19, so 1.14.0's programs 2 to 16 move up two, and 17 to 34 move up three. A saved session stores the preset's name as well as its number and is unaffected.

**Four presets sound different.** _6581 Triangle_, _Drawbar Organ_, _FM Piano_ and _Nylon EP_ are on their new characters, and _6581 Triangle_ has new levels and is 1 dB quieter. Every preset with a bus stage is slightly brighter in the top octave.

## Installing

The [install page](https://benjamindehli.github.io/overtonium/install/#downloads) has a row per file saying who each one is for. In short: take the installer for your platform, or the zip beside it if you have no administrator rights or keep plugins somewhere of your own.

| Platform | Installer              | Formats                      |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| macOS    | `.pkg`, with a chooser | VST3, Audio Unit, Standalone |
| Windows  | `.exe`                 | VST3, Standalone             |
| Linux    | none, use the zip      | VST3, LV2, Standalone        |

The macOS build is a universal binary and runs on both Apple Silicon and Intel. Logic and GarageBand only look for new Audio Units when they start, so restart them after installing.

On Linux the zip's contents go in `~/.vst3` and `~/.lv2`.

If you installed an earlier version with the package, the installer replaces it.

## What has actually been tried

All three platforms are built on every push, run through both test suites, and put through pluginval at strictness 8. The VST3 also goes through Steinberg's own conformance validator, and both suites run again under the address and undefined behaviour sanitizers. The new characters, temperaments and presets were played in a real host on macOS. Nobody has run the Windows or Linux builds in a DAW yet, so treat those as untried and please open an issue with whatever you find.
